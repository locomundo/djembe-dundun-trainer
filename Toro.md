# Toro — transcription

Read off `Toro.pdf`. Audio in `audio/toro-*.wav`. Legend as in [Djole.md](Djole.md).

**4/4 in sixteenths, two-bar cycle.** The only sheet with a written **entrance**
figure. Both dundun parts are genuine two-bar phrases.

## Entrance — played once

```
        1 e & a   2 e & a   3 e & a   4 e & a
        S . . .   S . S .   S . . .   S . S .
```

One bar of slaps to bring the ensemble in, then it drops out. Not a part to loop.

## Djembé 1 — one bar

```
        1 e & a   2 e & a   3 e & a   4 e & a
        T T . B   S . S .   S . . B   S . S .
```

The second bass, on the **a** of beat 3, is the one that catches people.

## Djembé 2 — half bar

```
        S . . S   S . T T
```

**Again the Fankani / Sunun Djembé 2** — the same eight slots on three sheets.

## Sangban — two bars, **begins at bar 2 beat 3**

```
  bar 1  T T . .   O . O .   O . . .   T . T .
  bar 2  T T . .   O . O .   O . . .   O . O .
  bell   ✕ ✕ . ✕   ✕ . ✕ .   ✕ . ✕ .   ✕ . ✕ .
```

Identical for three beats, then **bar 1 finishes on closed tones and bar 2 on
open ones**. That ending is the only difference, and it is easy to miss.

## Kenkeni — half bar

```
  drum  . . T T   . . O .
  bell  ✕ . ✕ ✕   . ✕ ✕ .
```

Nothing on the beat.

## Doundounba — two bars

```
  bar 1  drum  T T . .   . . . .   . . T T   . T T .
         bell  ✕ ✕ . ✕   ✕ . ✕ .   ✕ . ✕ ✕   . ✕ ✕ .
  bar 2  drum  T T . .   . . . .   . . . .   . . . .
         bell  ✕ ✕ . ✕   ✕ . ✕ .   ✕ . ✕ .   ✕ . ✕ .
```

Bar 1 answers its opening pair with a run across beats 3–4; **bar 2 plays the
pair and then nothing at all.** The bell changes with it.

## Practising the dunduns

| | Bell | Drum | Bell-only |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sangban** | 18 | 14 | **4** |
| **Kenkeni** | 20 | 12 | 8 |
| **Doundounba** | 19 | 8 | 11 |

Order: **sangban → kenkeni → doundounba**. The sangban has almost no
independence to build — its difficulty is purely remembering which bar you are
in. The doundounba's bar 2 is nearly all bell-only, so holding the bell steady
through that emptiness is the actual skill.
