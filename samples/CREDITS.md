## What these actually are

- **The three dunduns come from one recording session** (JIMMYJAMES112), so they
  sit together properly — matched room, mic and drums, and the pitches land in
  the right order: doundounba ≈ 108 Hz, sangban ≈ 172 Hz, kenkeni ≈ 215 Hz.
- **The djembe strokes come from three different drums**, because no single CC0
  set had a usable bass, tone *and* slap. They are each clearly the right stroke
  — that matters more for practice than matching timbre — but they will not
  sound like one instrument the way the dunduns do.
- The bell is an agogo rather than a true kenken. Closest CC0 relative.
