# Soli Lent — transcription

Read off `Soli Lent.pdf`. Audio in `audio/soli-lent-*.wav`. Legend as in
[Djole.md](Djole.md).

**4/4 in sixteenths, two-bar cycle.** Slow, and built on **two-bar djembe
phrases** rather than short loops. Uses the shared Djolé call.

## Djembé 1 — one bar

```
        1 e & a   2 e & a   3 e & a   4 e & a
        B . T T   . . S T   T . B S   . . S .
```

## Djembé 2 — one bar

```
        1 e & a   2 e & a   3 e & a   4 e & a
        S . . S   S . T T   S . B S   S . T T
```

Slap on every beat, answered by two tones on 2 and 4. The bass on beat 3 is the
only thing separating the halves.

## Sangban — one bar

```
  drum  T . . .   T . . .   T . O .   T . . .
  bell  ✕ . ✕ .   ✕ . ✕ .   ✕ . ✕ .   ✕ . ✕ .
```

A closed tone on every beat with one **open** tone on the & of beat 3 — the only
colour in the part.

## Kenkeni — half bar, **begins at bar 2 beat 4**

```
  drum  T . . .   . . T .
  bell  ✕ . ✕ ✕   . ✕ ✕ .
```

## Doundounba — two bars, three strokes in total

```
  bar 1  . . . .   . . . .   . . T .   . . . .
  bar 2  . . . .   . . . .   . . T .   T . . .
  bell   ✕ . ✕ ✕   . ✕ ✕ .   ✕ . ✕ .   ✕ . ✕ .
```

**Three strokes in the whole two-bar cycle.** The sparsest dundun part on any of
these sheets.

## Practising the dunduns

| | Bell | Drum | Bell-only |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sangban** | 16 | 8 | 8 |
| **Kenkeni** | 20 | 8 | 12 |
| **Doundounba** | 20 | **3** | **17** |

Order: **sangban → kenkeni → doundounba**. The doundounba has no coordination
pattern to learn — only the ability to hold the bell for two bars and place
three strokes accurately inside it.

Because it is slow, there is time to hear every stroke land early or late. Take
the tempo **down**, not up.
